## Team principles
- Students own the research, design decisions, content, and final implementation.
- Use AI to explain, critique, troubleshoot, and support learning—not to conceal who did the work.
- Verify every factual claim independently.
- Review and understand generated code before using it.
- Record AI-generated or AI-assisted text, images, code, and design elements as required by BPA policy.
- Do not upload private personal information or interview recordings to AI tools without appropriate permission.
