Get involved

Make recognition the beginning of action.

You don't need a title to make a difference. Start with one practical step.

01

Give your time

Find a local organization whose mission matches an issue you care about. Confirm its volunteer process and age requirements.

02

Share a skill

Offer a useful skill—tutoring, translation, design, organizing, or technology—through a trusted community group.

03

Recognize someone

Thank a person whose contribution is often overlooked. Ask permission before sharing their story or photograph publicly.
